# David Study — Business Plan & Commercial Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Company & Product**: **David Study** (Academic Mastery & Exam Preparation SaaS).
- **Core Mission**: Eliminate passive studying and guarantee university exam performance by transforming technical course materials into an active, 4-Phase Mastery Progression (Narrative Guide $\to$ Resetting Quiz $\to$ Practical Workbook with Writing Zone $\to$ Atomic Flashcards) backed by a 50-Minute Mock Exam Simulator.
- **Empirical Validation**:
  - Live pilot deployed for NOVA IMS university course (*Multivariate Data Analysis*).
  - **15 registered active students** (David, Manu, Biisca, Rodrigo, Mqnel, Miguel Silva, Caralho, Sean, Duarte, Soeiro, Pedro, Vasco, Henrique, Bisca, Miguel).
  - 80+ quiz sessions completed, 5 workbook submissions, and active mock exam simulations.
- **Core Competitive Moats**:
  1. *Syllabus Grounding*: 100% strictly calibrated to official professor lecture slides and practical workbooks.
  2. *Active Calculation*: Bridging the gap between multiple choice and real written exams via persistent writing zones and KaTeX worked solutions.
  3. *Turnkey AI Ingestion Engine*: Proprietary SOP capable of onboarding new university course syllabi in under 48 hours.

---

## 2. Problem Statement & Market Pain Points

1. **The "Passive Studying" Trap**: Students review 200+ slide PDFs passively, creating a false illusion of competence without testing true recall or mathematical calculation.
2. **Disconnected Problem Sets**: Lecture slides, homework sheets, and past exams live in fragmented PDFs without step-by-step guidance or interactive feedback.
3. **Flaws in Existing EdTech (Quizlet / Anki)**:
   - Designed for simple vocabulary flashcards.
   - Incapable of testing multi-part mathematical proofs, matrix calculations, or open-ended university problem sets.
4. **Verbosity Cues**: Standard online question banks allow students to guess the longest option without understanding the underlying concepts.

## 4. Target Market & Customer Personas

1. **Primary Segment: University STEM & Business Students (B2C)**
   - Undergraduates and Master's students in quantitative, rigorous fields: Data Science, Information Management, Econometrics, Computer Science, Finance, and Engineering.
   - High willingness to pay in the 3–4 weeks preceding midterm and final exams.
2. **Secondary Segment: Student Cohorts & Academic Societies (B2B2C)**
   - Academic clubs and class study groups purchasing group passes.
3. **Tertiary Segment: Academic Departments & Lecturers (B2B)**
   - Course instructors seeking an automated homework practice and exam prep engine that directly reflects their own syllabus.

## 6. Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

1. **Campus Seed & Cohort Flywheel (Proven Validation)**:
   - Initial deployment within NOVA IMS achieved 15 organic users through word-of-mouth.
   - Students share Preparation Scores, Sudden Death streaks, and exam results, creating organic viral loops.
2. **Syllabus-Specific Ingestion Velocity**:
   - Automated ingestion pipeline allows launching tailored exam preparation packs for specific university course codes right before exam season.
3. **The "Copy for AI" Organic Loop**:
   - The built-in "Copy for AI" button formats prompts for ChatGPT/Claude with platform attribution, spreading awareness across student WhatsApp and Discord groups.

## 7. Financial Model & Unit Economics

- **Infrastructure Cost per User**:
  - Hosting: Netlify CDN (essentially €0.00 at scale for static assets).
  - Database: Supabase PostgreSQL (free tier handles up to 50,000 MAU).
  - Estimated marginal server cost per active student: **< €0.05 / month**.
- **Gross Margin**: **> 95%** (pure software economics).
- **Revenue Milestones**:
  - **Year 1 (3 Universities, 5 Courses)**: 600 paying students $\times$ €30/semester = **€36,000 ARR**.
  - **Year 2 (10 Universities, 20 Courses)**: 3,500 paying students $\times$ €35/semester = **€245,000 ARR**.
  - **Year 3 (European Expansion, 50 Courses + B2B Licenses)**: 15,000 students + 20 institutional licenses = **€1,100,000 ARR**.
